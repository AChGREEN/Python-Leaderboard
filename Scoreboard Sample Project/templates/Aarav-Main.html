About the Game

Data Deal became a popular game by turning everyday online safety decisions into an entertaining challenge. Players are placed in realistic situations involving apps, permissions, emails, passwords, and suspicious online offers, then must decide whether to accept or reject each choice. The questions become more difficult as the game progresses, testing the player's attention, judgement, and understanding of cybersecurity while rewarding careful thinking with a higher score.

The game attracted a large community because it was easy to understand, enjoyable to replay, and created plenty of surprising moments. Its popularity grew even further when many streamers played it live, sharing their reactions, mistakes, and decisions with audiences around the world. Viewers could compare their own choices with the streamers' answers, which helped Data Deal reach an even wider audience.

How to Play

You will be presented with scenarios involving apps, permissions, emails, and security choices. Answer each question by pressing ACCEPT (green button) or REJECT (red button).

  • ACCEPT = the app/action is safe and legitimate
  • REJECT = the app/action is dangerous, fake, or a scam

A/D and Right/Left arrow can be used to Accept/Reject instead of using a mouse. Read the tips carefully after each question — they explain why your answer was correct or incorrect. When prompted to enter a password, enter a strong password above 15 characters. There is TTS for accessibility by default; if you don't want to listen to TTS, just play on mute.

Educational Purpose

This game educates players about which app permissions are legitimate and which are red flags. Players learn which permissions apps like Spotify, YouTube, and banking apps genuinely need versus apps that ask for excessive access. Tips explain why apps like “CoolKeyboard” (a keylogger) or “W1ndows Update” (a fake update) are dangerous.
  • Day 1 introduces foundational concepts (app safety)
  • Day 2 deepens understanding (real-world privacy scenarios)
  • Day 3 integrates all knowledge (passwords, phishing, scams)
  • Boss Fight (expert-level scenarios combining all three days)
Feedback Mechanisms: After every answer, players receive immediate feedback explaining the correct answer. This allows players to learn why they got it wrong and will be able to remember to use their knowledge in real life.

Gameplay

The game is played in rounds, with each round consisting of a series of questions. The questions become more difficult as the player progresses, testing their knowledge and decision-making skills. Players can earn points for correct answers and lose points for incorrect answers. The goal is to achieve the highest score possible by the end of the game.
